Clarifications

Client Enquiries & Chamber Protocols

What documents must I prepare before scheduling a preliminary consultation? +

For property disputes: bring the chain of title deeds (parent documents), current encumbrance certificate, patta copies, and municipal tax receipts. For civil or criminal matters: bring any summons, notices, charge sheets, plaints, or impugned orders issued by the court.

Do you accept online / video conferences for overseas or non-resident clients? +

Yes. We regularly conduct scheduled video conferences via Google Meet or Zoom for Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and corporate legal managers whose presence in Chennai is not immediately necessary.

How are retainers and professional fees structured? +

Fees are determined strictly according to the complexity, the forum of appearance (Supreme Court, High Court, Civil Court, or Tribunals), the volume of documents, and court drafting requirements. Full transparency is provided following initial brief inspection.

Can emergency bail applications be moved during court holidays or vacations? +

Yes. Urgent criminal matters, stay of demolition, or custody protections can be initiated before Vacation Benches of the Madras High Court or through duty magistrates as permitted by statutory court rules.
