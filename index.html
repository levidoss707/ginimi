Frequently Asked Questions

What records should I bring for an initial property consultation? +

Please bring the chronological chain of title deeds (parent documents), current encumbrance certificate (EC) from the Sub-Registrar Office, patta/chitta passbooks, approved layout plans, and any legal notices received from opposite parties or local authorities.

Do you accept video conferences for overseas or non-resident clients? +

Yes. Scheduled video conferences via Google Meet or Zoom are routinely conducted for Non-Resident Indians (NRIs), overseas investors, and corporate legal teams whose physical presence in Chennai is not immediately necessary.

Can emergency bail applications be moved during court vacations? +

Yes. Urgent criminal matters, anticipatory bails, and injunctions against arbitrary dispossession can be moved before Vacation Benches of the Madras High Court or duty magistrates according to statutory court vacation rules.
