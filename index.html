Clarifications

Client Inquiries & Chamber Protocols

What records are required for property disputes? +

Please bring the parent title deeds, current encumbrance certificate (EC), patta/chitta records, and any municipal notices or civil suits served upon you.

Do you provide video consultations for non-residents? +

Yes. Scheduled video conferences via Google Meet or Zoom are conducted for Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and corporate legal managers whose physical attendance is not immediately necessary.

Can emergency bail petitions be filed during court vacations? +

Yes. Urgent criminal matters, anticipatory bails, and injunctions against demolition can be initiated before Vacation Benches of the Madras High Court as per statutory rules.
